Stipendium Hungaricum — Fully Funded Scholarship
Pakistani students can apply through HEC Pakistan for full tuition + housing + monthly stipend + health insurance at Hungarian universities. Deadline: January 15 each year via HEC portal.

📋 Note on Your 12th Grade C

European universities generally evaluate your overall FSc aggregate across both 11th and 12th, not just the final year. Your strong A/A+ in 9th–11th helps. Universities in Poland and Hungary are most flexible — some even allow an entrance exam instead of relying on transcripts. Always include a motivation letter explaining the grade. Consider a Foundation Year at ELTE Budapest as a guaranteed entry pathway.
